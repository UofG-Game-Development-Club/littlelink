Privacy

This page is run by the Guelph Game Development Club at the University of Guelph. Here's everything it collects, which isn't much.

Visit and click counts

We count how many people open this page and which buttons they click, so we know what's useful and where people find us. We built this ourselves and it runs on our own Cloudflare account, nothing is shared with advertisers or analytics companies.

What we record

  • That the page was opened, or which button was clicked
  • Where you came from, like Instagram, a QR code or a search engine
  • Your country and whether you're on a phone, tablet or computer

What we don't record

  • No cookies and nothing stored on your device
  • No IP addresses, names, accounts or anything that identifies you

To count unique visitors, each visit gets an anonymous code made from a random value that's thrown away every day, so visits can't be linked from one day to the next or traced back to you. Only club executives can see the totals.

Hosting

The page is hosted by Cloudflare, which handles the connection to your browser. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Other sites

The buttons take you to sites like Discord, Instagram and LinkedIn. Once you're there, their own privacy policies apply.
